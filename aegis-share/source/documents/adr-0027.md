# 0027. The city generator is a pure field-and-subdivision engine in `src/lib/citygen/`

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-01

## Context

The product is a seed-deterministic cyberpunk city map simulator: terrain drives
zoning, zoning drives buildings, and the result renders in both a top-down 2D map
and a 3D view. Three architectures were designed independently and scored by three
adversarial judges on separate lenses (determinism/testability, visual
believability, extensibility/performance).

Two constraints decided the outcome, and neither was obvious at the start.

First, **`style-rules/no-loops` is an error over all of `src/`** and AGENTS.md
forbids silencing it. Every `for`, `for..of`, `while` and `do..while` is rejected;
only functional iteration and recursion remain. Measured in this repo on
2026-08-01: oxlint reports both `for` and `while`, while `Array.from(...).reduce`,
tail recursion and `Float32Array.map` pass. Measured the same day, maximum JS
recursion depth is ~9,765 frames against grids of up to 1,048,576 cells — so
per-cell recursion overflows the stack by two orders of magnitude, and folds are
the only per-cell instrument available.

Second, the panel's two road-generation candidates — RK4 tensor-streamline tracing
and Parish-Müller agent growth — are both unbounded sequential iteration over
mutable state. Their loop-free rewrites are 10^5-step tail recursions with no
engine TCO guarantee, and neither offers per-step testability: the judges
independently called the tensor version "a snapshot suite in disguise" and
predicted visible spaghetti where the grid and contour fields cancel.

## Decision

The generator is **"Strata/S"**: a stack of continuous scalar fields, discretised
by subdivision.

- Terrain synthesis produces elevation, then derived fields — slope, water
  distance, local eminence, flood risk, geodesic centrality, megatower shadow,
  prestige, decay. Every later decision samples these fields, so terrain reaches
  the city through one shared vocabulary rather than per-district special cases.
- Roads are **Dijkstra geodesic arterials** between field-derived anchors over a
  slope-and-water cost surface, so bridges emerge from the cost surface rather
  than being placed. Only these ~10-14 polylines are ever planarised.
- Blocks come from **orientation-field-guided recursive subdivision** of the faces
  the arterials enclose. Streets are cut segments of a subdivision tree, so they
  intersect nothing by construction and no dense street graph is ever
  face-walked. District borders are therefore always street edges.
- Every priority-queue algorithm runs through one `boundedDrain` primitive:
  recursion over chunks of 4,096 steps, giving a stack depth of
  `ceil(totalOps / 4096)` — about 512 frames for Dijkstra at the largest grid,
  against the ~9,765 measured limit.
- Determinism is a policy, not an aspiration: `Math.sin/cos/tan/exp/log/pow/atan2/
  hypot/cbrt/random`, `**`, and the clock are banned inside the engine; noise,
  PRNG and hashing are in-repo; every heap key ends in a unique integer so ties
  never fall to ambient array order. Golden per-stage content hashes are the proof,
  a source scan is the guard.

The engine lives in **`src/lib/citygen/`**. ADR-0016 describes `src/lib/**` as
framework and infrastructure adapters plus generic values with no component
affinity; a pure domain engine is not literally any of those, and this ADR extends
that layer to cover it. The test applied is `.claude/rules/react.md`'s: a web of
pure functions with zero component affinity that could ship as a library. It
imports only `src/entities/city/`.

Rendering uses one `three/webgpu` `WebGPURenderer` (automatic WebGL2 fallback) over
a single scene; the 2D map is an orthographic camera and the 3D view a perspective
camera over that same scene, so switching views swaps a camera rather than
rebuilding geometry.

## Alternatives considered

- **Tensor-field streamline roads (the strongest visual proposal).** Rejected: no
  honest loop-free formulation, untestable per-step, and an unspecified degenerate
  case. The idea survives in a bounded role — it selects subdivision cut angles,
  with an explicit tested fallback when the blended magnitude is near zero.
- **Parish-Müller growth.** Rejected for the same iteration shape, despite
  producing the most organic street patterns in the panel.
- **Strict hierarchical partition as the city structure.** Rejected: it produces
  visible cellular seams and no through-roads. Its recursive OBB splitter is kept
  as a subroutine for lots and block infill.
- **Engine under `src/components/features/city-map/`.** Rejected: colocation would
  put a pure, testable, component-free engine inside the presentation layer, and
  moving it later when server-side generation arrives is a real refactor.
- **Engine under `src/entities/city/`.** Rejected: ADR-0016 scopes entities to
  domain types and schemas. The vocabulary lives there; the algorithms do not.
- **A loop-friendly escape hatch** (an oxlint override for the engine directory).
  Rejected: the constraint produced a better architecture here, not merely a
  compliant one, and an exemption would have preserved the untestable design.

## Consequences

- Adding a district archetype is additive: a member on a `const` array plus a row
  in the affinity and massing tables, which fail to compile until handled.
- The engine is unit-testable to 100 % branch coverage because every stage is a
  pure total function and no stage traces an unbounded trajectory.
- Cost: the loop-free style is unidiomatic. A contributor writing an obvious `for`
  gets a lint error rather than guidance, and `boundedDrain`'s chunked recursion is
  harder to read than the `while` loop it replaces. This ADR is where that trade is
  recorded.
- Cost: banning `pow`/`exp` means distributions are shaped by explicit branches and
  rational falloffs. Retuning the skyline means editing a branch, not an exponent.
- Cross-engine reproducibility is designed for but only opportunistically tested;
  CI goldens are single-engine in the first slice.
- The generation lifecycle is interaction-complex and carries its own verified
  state machine in `specs/city-generation.spec.md` (ADR-0010/0015), separate from
  this record.
